## #1 CapCut
- Website: https://www.capcut.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Edit shorts and reels with auto captions
- In plain English: A free, easy video editor made for TikTok-style videos. Auto captions, templates and simple cuts are on the free plan; some effects need Pro.

## #2 Opus Clip
- Website: https://www.opus.pro
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Turn a long video or stream into short clips
- In plain English: Upload a long video or paste a YouTube link and AI picks the best moments, crops them vertical and adds captions. Free plan gives 60 minutes of video a month, with a watermark.

## #3 Gemini Omni (formerly Veo)
- Website: https://gemini.google/overview/video-generation/
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: Realistic AI video clips with sound, made and edited by chatting
- In plain English: Google's video maker in the Gemini app, which replaced Veo. Describe a scene or upload photos and it makes a realistic clip with sound, then you can ask for changes. Needs a paid Google AI plan.

## #4 Kling AI
- Website: https://kling.ai
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Make AI video clips from text or a picture
- In plain English: Makes smooth, realistic clips with good lip-sync at a low price. The free plan lets you try it (watermarked, personal use only), and paid plans start under $10 a month.

## #5 Descript
- Website: https://www.descript.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Edit video by editing the transcript
- In plain English: Your video becomes a text document. Delete words in the text and they're cut from the video. Good for talking videos and podcasts.

## #6 Runway
- Website: https://runway.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Generate short AI video clips from text or images
- In plain English: Makes brand-new video clips from a description or a picture. Free plan is a one-time 125 credits, enough to try a few clips.

## #7 Grok Imagine
- Website: https://grok.com/imagine
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: Short AI video clips with sound from text or a picture
- In plain English: Turns a description or a picture into a short clip with sound effects and even talking characters. Video needs the $30/month Premium plan, so try Runway's free credits first.

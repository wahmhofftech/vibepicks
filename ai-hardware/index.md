## #1 Arduino Cloud
- Website: https://cloud.arduino.cc
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Write, upload and control Arduino/ESP32 projects with a built-in AI assistant
- In plain English: Arduino's own online editor has an AI helper that writes and fixes the code for your board, and lets you control wireless (Wi-Fi) projects from your phone. Free plan includes 30 AI requests a month.

## #2 Wokwi
- Website: https://wokwi.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Simulate Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi Pico projects in your browser
- In plain English: Build and test your circuit on screen with virtual boards, sensors and LEDs, including Wi-Fi on the ESP32, so you can't fry real parts while learning. Free for public projects.

## #3 Tinkercad
- Website: https://www.tinkercad.com
- Pricing: Free
- Use it for: Beginner circuits and 3D-printable designs
- In plain English: Autodesk's free, super-friendly tool for designing parts to 3D print and wiring up practice circuits with a virtual Arduino.

## #4 ChatGPT
- Website: https://chatgpt.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Explain wiring, write firmware and troubleshoot step by step
- In plain English: Describe your parts and what you want them to do and it explains the wiring and writes the board code. You can even send it a photo of your setup when something isn't working.

## #5 Flux
- Website: https://www.flux.ai
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: AI-assisted circuit and PCB design
- In plain English: An AI helper for designing your own custom circuit board, from parts list to a board you can order. Paid after a 14-day free trial (free for students).

## #6 OpenPLC
- Website: https://autonomylogic.com
- Pricing: Free
- Use it for: Free, open-source PLC you can program with ladder logic
- In plain English: Learn and test PLC programs (ladder logic, structured text) for free without buying an industrial controller. It can run on a regular PC or a Raspberry Pi.

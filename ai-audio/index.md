## #2 ElevenLabs
- Website: https://elevenlabs.io
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Realistic AI voiceovers, narration, sound effects and music

## #3 Descript
- Website: https://www.descript.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Edit podcasts and videos by editing the transcript
- In plain English: Turns your recording into text, and you edit the audio just like a document: delete a word and it's gone from the recording. Free plan includes 60 minutes a month.

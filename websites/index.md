## #1 Wix
- Website: https://www.wix.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: AI-built business websites with drag-and-drop editing
- In plain English: Describe your business and its AI builds a starter site you can then drag and edit. Free plan works but shows Wix ads and a wixsite.com address.

## #2 Shopify
- Website: https://www.shopify.com
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: Online store to sell products
- In plain English: The standard for selling things online: products, checkout, shipping and payments in one place. Short free trial, then usually $1 a month for 3 months before regular pricing.

## #3 Framer
- Website: https://www.framer.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Good-looking sites and landing pages with AI layouts
- In plain English: Great for a sharp-looking landing page or portfolio. AI can draft the layout for you. Free plan uses a Framer address and badge.

## #4 Squarespace
- Website: https://www.squarespace.com
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: Polished templates for a simple business site or shop
- In plain English: Clean, professional templates that are hard to make look bad. No free plan, but there's a 14-day free trial.

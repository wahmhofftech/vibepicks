## #1 ChatGPT
- Website: https://chatgpt.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Draft posts, emails and product descriptions
- In plain English: Tell it what you're selling and who it's for and it writes a first draft in seconds. Ask for a few versions and pick the one that sounds most like you.

## #2 Claude
- Website: https://claude.ai
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Natural-sounding writing and rewrites
- In plain English: Good at writing that doesn't sound like a robot, and at rewriting your own draft to be clearer. Free plan has daily limits.

## #3 Gemini
- Website: https://gemini.google.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Drafts and rewrites, built into Gmail and Google Docs
- In plain English: Google's AI assistant. Good for first drafts and rewrites, and handy if you live in Gmail and Google Docs, since paid plans put it right inside them. The free plan has everyday limits.

## #4 Grok
- Website: https://grok.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Timely social posts and replies
- In plain English: A chat assistant like ChatGPT that can read live posts on X and the web, so it's handy for writing about what people are talking about today. The free plan has limited daily use; the $8/month Plus plan gives more.

## #5 Grammarly
- Website: https://www.grammarly.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Fix spelling, grammar and tone as you type
- In plain English: Checks your writing everywhere you type, in email, social posts and docs. The free plan catches spelling and grammar mistakes.

## #1 Playwright
- Website: https://playwright.dev
- Pricing: Free
- Use it for: Reliable end-to-end browser tests (open source)
- In plain English: Free tool that clicks through your site like a real person to catch anything that breaks.

## #2 Cypress
- Website: https://www.cypress.io
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Developer-friendly E2E and component testing
- In plain English: Lets you watch automated tests click through your app so you can see exactly where bugs happen.

## #3 Sentry
- Website: https://sentry.io
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Catching errors and crashes in production
- In plain English: Emails you when your live app crashes and shows exactly what went wrong.

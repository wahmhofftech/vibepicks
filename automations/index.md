## #1 Zapier
- Website: https://zapier.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Connect apps with simple if-this-then-that steps
- In plain English: The easiest place to start: pick a trigger and an action and it just runs. Free plan covers 100 tasks a month with two-step automations.

## #2 Make
- Website: https://www.make.com
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Visual drag-and-drop automations
- In plain English: Draw your automation as a flowchart. More free room than Zapier (1,000 credits a month), a little more to learn.

## #3 n8n
- Website: https://n8n.io
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Powerful automations and AI agents you can host yourself
- In plain English: Very flexible and free if you run it on your own computer or server. The hosted version is a 14-day trial, then paid. Best once you're comfortable.

## #4 Grok Bot
- Website: https://x.ai/bot
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: AI teammates that work in your apps and run routines on a schedule
- In plain English: Instead of building a flowchart, you tell it the job in plain English and it signs into your tools and does it, even on a schedule. VibePicks is built and run with it. It starts at $30/month, and simple fixed tasks are cheaper and more predictable in Zapier or Make.

# Best AI Images & Art for Vibe Coders (2026)
> Ranked editorial picks from VibePicks. #1 is our top pick. Rankings are never sold; sponsored spots are always labeled.

Page: https://vibepicks.ai/ai-images/
Last updated: 2026-10-26

## #1 ChatGPT Images
- Website: https://chatgpt.com/images
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Quick images and edits by chatting
- In plain English: Just describe the picture in plain English and ask for changes like "make it smaller and add a blue outline". Easiest place to start.

## #2 Nano Banana (Gemini)
- Website: https://gemini.google/overview/image-generation/
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Make and edit images by chatting in the Gemini app
- In plain English: Google's image maker inside Gemini. Great at editing your own photos, keeping a character looking the same, and putting clear words in pictures. Free to use; a Google AI plan unlocks the best model and higher limits.

## #3 Midjourney
- Website: https://www.midjourney.com
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: High-quality illustrations and artistic images
- In plain English: Produces some of the most beautiful, artistic images around. No free plan, so try a free option first.

## #4 FLUX (Black Forest Labs)
- Website: https://bfl.ai
- Pricing: Paid
- Use it for: Photo-real images and an image API with open models
- In plain English: Makes very realistic photos and product shots, and it's one of the cheapest to use in your own app (pay per image, no subscription). Some versions are free to download and run on a powerful computer.

## #5 Ideogram
- Website: https://ideogram.ai
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Logos, stickers, posters and images with readable text
- In plain English: The best at putting real, readable words inside a picture, so it's great for logos, thumbnails and channel art. Free credits every week.

## #6 Grok Imagine
- Website: https://grok.com/imagine
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Quick images from a sentence, right inside Grok
- In plain English: Type what you want and it makes pictures in seconds, then you can ask for changes. The free plan includes a limited number of images a day (xAI doesn't say how many).

## #7 Recraft
- Website: https://www.recraft.ai
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Logos, icons, vector art and brand sets
- In plain English: Makes clean logos and icons that stay sharp at any size, so they look good as tiny emotes and on big banners alike.

## #8 Leonardo
- Website: https://leonardo.ai
- Pricing: Freemium
- Use it for: Emotes, game assets, characters and stylized art
- In plain English: Lots of ready-made art styles for cartoons, game items and characters, which makes it easy to get a consistent look for a set of emotes. Free tokens every day.

Quick answers

What is the best pick in Payments & Email for 2026?

Stripe is our #1 pick in Payments & Email: accept card payments, subscriptions and checkout links. The standard way to take payments online. Make a payment link with no code, or add checkout to your app. No monthly fee: 2.9% + 30¢ per US card sale.

Is there a free option in Payments & Email?

Yes. Resend is free to start, with paid upgrades. The easiest way for an app to send emails, and AI coding tools know it well. Free for 3,000 emails a month (100 a day); $20/month for 50,000.

What are the best alternatives to Stripe?

In our ranking: #2 Resend (send emails from your app: receipts, sign-ups, password resets); #3 Paddle (sell software and subscriptions with sales tax handled for you); #4 Polar (sell digital products, licenses and subscriptions, tax handled).
